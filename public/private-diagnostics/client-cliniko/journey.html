Determining global behavioral impact based on Trust Profile...

Strategic Isolation

Buyers landing directly on Commercial Intent bypass earlier trust-building. If Structural Authority is weak, the exit is binary and instant. You lose the buyer to a competitor with better perceived stability.

Compounding Evaluation

Early-stage buyers (Search/Evaluate) may cycle out and return. However, unresolved risks compound — a buyer may accept weak Layer 1 signals temporarily, but they will never convert if those gaps remain at the decision stage.
