Entry Territories

  • Some buyers first encounter the platform through a regulatory or compliance search.

  • Others arrive through workflow, feature, or implementation searches.

  • Others enter through authority and comparison territory.

  • Some buyers enter through proof, reputation, or validation searches.

Why This Matters

Whichever layer a buyer enters through becomes their first trust test. If that entry layer is already weak, confidence drops before the rest of the system has a chance to compensate.
