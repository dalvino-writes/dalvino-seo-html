How to Read This Diagnostic

What this report is showing you

This orientation block explains what the score means, how the pages connect, and how to interpret trust leakage before you move deeper into the diagnostic.

1 · What the score means

The total score summarizes trust strength across four layers. Lower scores do not mean “bad product”; they signal where trust is under-communicated or under-validated before sales engagement.

2 · What trust leakage means

Trust leakage is the estimated share of qualified buyer confidence that drops out before entering sales because proof, structure, or validation signals do not fully stabilize evaluation.

3 · What the next pages do

The next pages translate the score into buyer-friction stages, commercial impact, and strategic recovery priorities so the trust deficit becomes operationally visible.

4 · What this is not

This is a strategic trust diagnostic. It is not a legal certification, regulatory opinion, or substitute for implementation planning. It is designed to clarify where buyer confidence is currently weakening.
